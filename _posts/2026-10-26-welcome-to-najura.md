Our little studio has a new name: **Najura**.

We started out making puzzle games under the name Naj Studio. As we began planning apps as well as games, we wanted one name that could cover everything we make. Najura is that name, and [najura.com](https://najura.com/) is its new home.

## What changes for you

Nothing you need to do. Your games, your progress and your saved levels all stay exactly the same. The only thing that changes is the name you'll see on the store page and on this website.

## Where to find everything

- **[games.najura.com](https://games.najura.com/)**: all our games, with screenshots and download links.
- **[app.najura.com](https://app.najura.com/)**: our upcoming apps.
- **blog.najura.com**: you're here! Tips, guides and news.

## What's next

[Peep](https://games.najura.com/#peep), our little talking chick, is almost ready. After that, we're working on more puzzle games, our first apps, and iPhone and iPad versions of our games.

Thanks for playing, and welcome to Najura!
