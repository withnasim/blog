<h1>About Najura</h1>
<div class="prose" markdown="1">
Najura is the games and apps brand of Najuvia LLC, a small independent company. We make cozy puzzle games and simple, friendly apps.

We build things we'd want to use ourselves: easy to learn, beautiful to look at, and kind to your time and privacy. Our games work offline, download fast and never ask you to make an account.

**Our games:** [Brickwise](https://games.najura.com/#brickwise), [Potion Pour](https://games.najura.com/#potionpour) and [Peep](https://games.najura.com/#peep) (coming soon). They're on Google Play today, and iPhone and iPad versions are planned.

**Support:** questions, feedback or found a bug? Email us at [info@najura.com](mailto:info@najura.com).
</div>
