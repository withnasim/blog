<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>

This policy covers the Najura Blog at blog.najura.com. Each of our games and apps has its own privacy policy, linked from [games.najura.com](https://games.najura.com/).

**What we collect.** The blog has no accounts, comments or forms, so we don't ask you for any personal information.

**Advertising.** If we show ads on this blog in the future, we'll update this policy first to explain how ad partners such as Google may use cookies.
