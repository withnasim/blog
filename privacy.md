<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
<div class="prose" markdown="1">
*Last updated: October 26, 2026*

This policy covers the Najura Blog at blog.najura.com. Each of our games and apps has its own privacy policy, linked from [games.najura.com](https://games.najura.com/).

**What we collect.** The blog has no accounts, comments or forms, so we don't ask you for any personal information.

**Hosting.** The blog is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

**Analytics.** We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, on this blog and our other najura.com pages to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type. It does not tell us who you are. You can opt out with the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). See [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Fonts.** Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the font is downloaded.

**Advertising.** If we show ads on this blog in the future, we'll update this policy first to explain how ad partners such as Google may use cookies.

**Contact.** Questions? Email our support team at [info@najura.com](mailto:info@najura.com).
</div>
